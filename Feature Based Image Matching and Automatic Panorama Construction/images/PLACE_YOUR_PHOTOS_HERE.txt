Place three or more overlapping photographs in this folder to run the system on
your own images instead of the scene photograph.

Naming: alphabetical order must match left-to-right order, e.g.
    01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg, 04.jpg

For good results:
  * overlap neighbouring shots by roughly 30-50%
  * rotate the camera about its own axis rather than walking sideways
    (translation plus scene depth breaks the homography assumption -- see
    Section 11.1 of the report)
  * lock exposure and focus if your camera allows it

The system detects these files automatically on the next run of
    python src/main.py

Note: in real mode there is no ground truth, so the alignment-error and
matching-precision columns become "n/a". The overlap-consistency metrics
(RMSE, NCC) still work.
